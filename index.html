School of Social and Political Science

Neuropolitics Research Lab

We are a transdisciplinary lab using cutting-edge methods to understand political attitudes, identities, and decision-making. We investigate the relationship between neuro-biological processes and higher order social behaviour.

What we do Meet our team

Our research

Four programmes, one question

What is happening in people's brains, minds and bodies when they post comments online, make policy, vote in elections, or decide whether to trust or share ‘fake’ news?

Political Behaviour

Measuring spontaneous expression of opinion online alongside the neuro-biological correlates of political behaviour.

Public Policy

How the policy environment, physiology, personality and inter-group dynamics shape policy decisions and public acceptance.

Identity

What underpins a sense of social belonging and political obligation, and how implicit triggers shift it.

Political Communication

How citizens balance accuracy against consistency in an age of ‘fake news’ and bots.

Our methods

From the scanner to the timeline

Our team combines functional brain imaging and psychophysiology with survey experiments, behavioural games and large-scale computational text analysis — including large language models — to explore the mind–brain–action nexus in political context.

We use these insights to guide public policy practice and enhance public understanding at a national and international level.
