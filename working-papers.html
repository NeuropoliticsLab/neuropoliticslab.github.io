Working papers & preprints

Work that is under review, posted as a preprint, registered, or in preparation. These are living documents — versions on OSF, PsyArXiv and SSRN may be more current than what is described here. Published work is listed on the publications page.

A note on preprints

The lab posts preprints and pre-registrations as a matter of course. Where a study has been registered, the registration is the authoritative statement of the design and analysis plan — not the working paper, and not this page. Data and analysis code are deposited alongside published papers where the study design allows it.

If a working paper here has since appeared in a journal, it will have moved to the publications page.
