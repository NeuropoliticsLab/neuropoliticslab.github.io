Public Engagement

Teaching, press and public understanding

We use our findings to guide public policy practice and enhance public understanding at a national and international level.

Teaching and supervision

We convene and teach several courses across Politics and International Relations and the Edinburgh Futures Institute, including Neuropolitics, and the Neuropolitics of Decision Making.

Members of the lab supervise doctoral and masters students across politics, psychology and computational social science. Supervisory interests include European identity, banal Europeanism, neuropolitics, physiology and behaviour in relation to identity, implicit identity triggers, experimental approaches to identity and policy, and EU social policy, civil society, governance and the EU policy process.

Several members welcome supervision enquiries — see the team page for individual interests.
